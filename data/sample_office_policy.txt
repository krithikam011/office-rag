ACME CORP — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

1. LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per calendar year, accrued
monthly at 1.5 days/month. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried
forward to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3
working days in advance via the HR portal, except in emergencies.

2. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior manager
approval. Full remote work requires director-level sign-off and is
evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

3. REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Travel, internet, and client-meeting expenses are reimbursable up to
Rs. 5,000/month with valid receipts. Reimbursement claims must be filed
within 30 days of the expense through the finance portal.

4. PROBATION POLICY
New employees are on probation for the first 6 months. Confirmation is
based on performance review by the reporting manager. During probation,
the notice period for resignation is 15 days; after confirmation, it
increases to 60 days.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism in all communications.
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and can be reported
confidentially to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

6. IT AND SECURITY POLICY
Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled at all times. Personal
devices used for work (BYOD) must be registered with IT and have MDM
(Mobile Device Management) software installed. Passwords must be changed
every 90 days.
